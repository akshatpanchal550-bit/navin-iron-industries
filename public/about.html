Who we are

Steel work, kept close to the source

Navin Iron Industries is a metal fabrication business based in Dungarpur, Rajasthan. We cut, weld and finish steel for homes, sites and industry — gates, railings, structural pieces and custom jobs that don't fit off a catalogue.

Cutting, welding and finishing all happen in the same shop, so a job doesn't wait on outside vendors to move forward.
